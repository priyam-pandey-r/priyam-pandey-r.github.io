About Priyam Pandey

Undergraduate Researcher · School of Data Science, IISER Thiruvananthapuram

DIL Lab · advised by Dr. Alwin Poulose

I am currently pursuing a BS-MS Dual Degree in Data Science at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Thiruvananthapuram (IISER TVM). At the DIL Lab, advised by Dr. Alwin Poulose, I conduct research in multimodal representation learning, 3D geometric deep learning, and macromolecular foundation models.

My research focuses on building generalizable representation learning systems for complex biological data. I bridge continuous 3D Euclidean coordinates, discrete topological graph structures, and high-dimensional evolutionary language representations to build robust, generalizable foundation models for biological discovery.
