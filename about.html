About Priyam Pandey

Undergraduate Researcher · School of Data Science, IISER Thiruvananthapuram

I am currently pursuing a BS-MS Dual Degree in Data Science at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Thiruvananthapuram (IISER TVM). I conduct research at the DIL Lab under the supervision of Dr. Alwin Poulose.

My research focuses on formulating mathematically disciplined representations for complex biological entities. I bridge continuous 3D Euclidean coordinates, discrete topological graph structures, and high-dimensional evolutionary language representations to build robust, generalizable foundation models for biological discovery.
