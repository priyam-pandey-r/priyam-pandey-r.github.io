About Priyam Pandey

Undergraduate Researcher · School of Data Science, IISER Thiruvananthapuram

DIL Lab · advised by Dr. Alwin Poulose

I am a third-year BS–MS student at the School of Data Science, IISER Thiruvananthapuram, developing my research interests at the intersection of machine learning and computational biology. At the DIL Lab, advised by Dr. Alwin Poulose, I conduct research in multimodal representation learning and biological foundation models.

My primary research interest is representation learning for complex biological systems—understanding how machine learning models can learn informative representations from diverse biological data and use them for scientific prediction and generative modelling. I am particularly interested in multimodal representation learning, biological foundation models, and geometric deep learning, with an emphasis on integrating complementary data modalities and capturing meaningful structural, relational, and functional information.

My current projects explore these questions across protein modelling, cellular perturbation prediction, and neuronal morphology. I also have experience investigating multimodal learning and reliability-aware fusion in human behaviour recognition, providing a broader methodological perspective on learning from complementary and potentially noisy data sources.

Currently Exploring
