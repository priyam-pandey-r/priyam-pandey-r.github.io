ProteinFM: Adaptive Multimodal Protein Foundation Models

Priyam Pandey · Manuscript in Preparation

Focus: Multimodal protein representation learning

My contribution: Designed and evaluated adaptive fusion architectures for sequence–structure representation learning.

Unimodal protein representations face inherent biophysical trade-offs: sequence representations (ESM-2, ProtT5) excel at capturing evolutionary semantics and distant homology, but lack explicit 3D spatial coordinate geometry. Conversely, structural representations (GearNet, Foldseek) capture tertiary geometry and active site pockets, but are bounded by experimental noise and low-confidence predicted regions.

Architecture Pipeline:
Sequence Modality ESM-2 / ProtT5
+
3D Coordinate Graph GearNet / Foldseek
→
Adaptive Fusion pLDDT Gated
→
Unified Representation
Sequence Modality ESM-2 / ProtT5
↓
3D Coordinate Graph GearNet / Foldseek
↓
Adaptive Fusion (pLDDT Gated)
↓
Unified Representation

Morphogenesis Fusion: Multi-View Representation Learning for Neuronal Morphology

Priyam Pandey, Alwin Poulose · Under Review in Neurocomputing (Elsevier)

Focus: Multi-view neuronal morphology learning

My contribution: Developed multimodal fusion across graph, point-cloud, and voxel representations.

Reconstructed 3D neuronal arborizations display rich continuous spatial distributions and intricate discrete branching topologies. We designed a tri-modal framework combining PointNet++ (point clouds), GNNs (SWC branching trees), and 3D CNNs (voxel grids).

Tri-Modal Processing:

+
Graph Tree PNA / GAT
+
3D Voxel Grid 3D CNN
→
Morphogenesis Fusion

↓

↓
Morphogenesis Fusion

Multimodal Learning With Thermal Imagery and Pose Cues for Privacy-Preserving Behavior Recognition

Priyam Pandey, Alwin Poulose · Knowledge-Based Systems (Elsevier)

Focus: Multimodal behavior recognition

My contribution: Formulated Confidence-Gated Cross-Attention (CGCA) and Cross-Gated Hierarchical Cross-Attention (CGHCA); 5-seed statistical validation.

Introduced Confidence-Gated Cross-Attention (CGCA) and Cross-Gated Hierarchical Cross-Attention (CGHCA) to regulate cross-modal attention maps dynamically based on observational confidence metrics under sensor noise, body occlusion, and thermal drift.
