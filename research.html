ProteinFM: Adaptive Multimodal Protein Foundation Models

Priyam Pandey · Manuscript in Preparation

Focus: Multimodal protein representation learning

Personal Contribution: Designed parameter-efficient adapters (PAEP), implemented confidence-gated adaptive fusion (APF) conditioned on pLDDT scores, and engineered the 9-stage U0–U8 probing benchmark.

Unimodal protein representations face inherent biophysical trade-offs: sequence representations (ESM-2, ProtT5) excel at capturing evolutionary semantics and distant homology, but lack explicit 3D spatial coordinate geometry. Conversely, structural representations (GearNet, Foldseek) capture tertiary geometry and active site pockets, but are bounded by experimental noise and low-confidence predicted regions.

Architecture Pipeline:
Sequence Modality ESM-2 / ProtT5
+
3D Coordinate Graph GearNet / Foldseek
→
PAEP + APF Fusion pLDDT Uncertainty Gating
→
U0–U8 Probing Suite Biophysical Validation

Core Architectural Components:

  • PAEP (Parameter-Efficient Adapter-Encoder-Projector): Projects frozen representations into a dimensionally aligned latent metric manifold.
  • APF (Adaptive Poly-modal Fusion): Dynamically shifts attention toward evolutionary sequence representations when structural confidence (pLDDT) drops.
  • U0–U8 Diagnostic Probing: 9 diagnostic axes evaluating biological similarity, GO functional ontology, mutation fitness landscapes, and 3D contact recovery.

Morphogenesis Fusion: Multi-View Representation Learning for Neuronal Morphology

Priyam Pandey, Alwin Poulose · Under Review in Neurocomputing (Elsevier)

Focus: 3D geometric deep learning for biological morphology

Personal Contribution: First author. Developed the tri-modal geometric pipeline combining point clouds, GNN branching graphs, and voxel grids; formulated the Morphogenesis Fusion gating network.

Reconstructed 3D neuronal arborizations display rich continuous spatial distributions and intricate discrete branching topologies. We designed a tri-modal framework combining:

Tri-Modal Processing:
3D Point Clouds PointNet++ (Curvature)
+
Arborization Graphs PNA / GAT (Topology)
+
Volumetric Grid 3D CNN (Orientation)
→
Morphogenesis Fusion Leakage-Free Validation

Our Morphogenesis Fusion module hierarchically aligns these complementary views to achieve superior accuracy in biological subtype classification and morphometric phenotyping.
