ProteinFM: Adaptive Multimodal Protein Foundation Models

Priyam Pandey · Manuscript in Preparation

Unimodal protein representations face inherent biophysical trade-offs: sequence/evolutionary representations (ESM-2, ProtT5) excel at capturing evolutionary conservation and distant homology across millions of uncurated sequences, but lack explicit 3D spatial coordinate geometry. Conversely, structural representations (GearNet, Foldseek) capture tertiary geometry and active site pockets, but are bounded by experimental noise and low-confidence predicted regions.

Architecture Pipeline:
1. Inputs: 1D Sequence (ESM-2) + 3D Coordinates (GearNet) + AlphaFold pLDDT Scores
2. Adapters: Parameter-Efficient Adapter-Encoder-Projector (PAEP)
3. Fusion: Adaptive Poly-modal Fusion (APF) with confidence-conditioned gating
4. Probing: 9-stage U0–U8 Systematic Evaluation Suite

Morphogenesis Fusion: Multi-View Representation Learning for Neuronal Morphology

Priyam Pandey, Alwin Poulose · Under Review in Neurocomputing (Elsevier)

Reconstructed 3D neuronal arborizations display rich continuous spatial distributions and intricate discrete branching topologies. We designed a tri-modal framework combining:

  • PointNet++: Encodes local geometric curvature and coordinate density distributions.
  • Graph Neural Networks (PNA/GAT): Models dendritic branching graphs and axonal tree topologies.
  • 3D Volumetric CNN: Encodes gross spatial bounding volumes and directional orientation.

Our Morphogenesis Fusion module hierarchically aligns these views to achieve superior accuracy in biological subtype classification under strict data-leakage controls.

Multimodal Learning With Thermal Imagery and Pose Cues for Privacy-Preserving Behavior Recognition

Priyam Pandey, Alwin Poulose · Knowledge-Based Systems (Elsevier)

Introduced Confidence-Gated Cross-Attention (CGCA) and Cross-Gated Hierarchical Cross-Attention (CGHCA) to regulate cross-modal attention maps dynamically based on observational confidence metrics under sensor noise, body occlusion, and thermal drift. Evaluated with 5-seed statistical validation.
