ProteinFM: Adaptive Multimodal Protein Foundation Models

Parameter-efficient adapters (PAEP) and adaptive poly-modal fusion (APF) dynamically integrating sequence (ESM-2, SaProt) and structure (GearNet) representations under pLDDT uncertainty gating. Evaluated via a rigorous 9-stage U0–U8 systematic probing protocol.
