DIL Lab · advised by Dr. Alwin Poulose

My research focuses on building generalizable representation learning systems for complex biological data, with applications across protein science, cellular morphology, and multimodal biological intelligence.

I design principled neural architectures for learning from heterogeneous biological data—spanning 1D evolutionary sequences, 3D point clouds, branching graphs, and volumetric densities.

Research Direction

My research explores how representation learning can unify heterogeneous biological information. I am particularly focused on three complementary areas:

Protein AI

Integrating evolutionary sequence representations with 3D structural geometry and biophysical confidence constraints.

Multimodal Biological AI

Developing principled fusion architectures that dynamically weight heterogeneous modalities based on observational certainty.

Geometric Deep Learning

Graph neural networks, point cloud models, and SE(3) representations capturing spatial curvature and branching topology.
